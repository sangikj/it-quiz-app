Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026 • Developer: Sangarya

Sangarya IT Quiz ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed as an educational interview preparation tool. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding how the application operates.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require account registration, login credentials, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device location.

2. Third-Party Services

The app connects over encrypted HTTPS to Supabase solely to fetch public interview quiz questions and explanations. Standard network metadata (such as IP addresses) may be processed transiently by our cloud infrastructure provider to deliver content securely, and is not used to identify or track individual users.

3. Data Security & Children's Privacy

All network communication uses industry-standard TLS encryption. Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, it is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce user accounts, leaderboards, or analytics in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before those features go live.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, please reach out via our official GitHub repository or developer support contact listed on Google Play.
